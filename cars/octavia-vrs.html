04Watch-outs

  • CBU parts lead times

    As a 200-unit CBU import, some RS-specific parts are import-dependent rather than stocked like the locally assembled Octavia's. Factor lead time into any repair.

  • Wet-clutch DSG service

    The DQ381 is a wet-clutch unit with its own service schedule — a different profile from the dry-clutch DQ200. Check the gearbox service record on any used car.

  • Thin used supply

    Only 200 cars were sold new. Published used listings (CarDekho, checked November 2026) showed 2021 cars in Bengaluru at ₹16.47 lakh (93,348 km) and ₹19.75 lakh (73,572 km) — individual listings, not a surveyed market range.
