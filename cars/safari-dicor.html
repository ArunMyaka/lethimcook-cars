04Watch-outs

  • Timing belt is time-critical

    Ownership reports collated by Team-BHP advise replacing the belt, tensioners, idlers and water pump at about 5–6 years regardless of low mileage, because belt failure is destructive to this engine.

  • Clutch master cylinder

    A recurring report in the Safari 2.2 technical thread: clutch pedal not returning, traced to the master cylinder.

  • Leaks and smoke

    Steering-oil and engine-oil leaks appear in early ownership reports; excessive black smoke and EGR checks, plus erratic cold-start idling, are recurring thread topics.

  • Age and fitness rules

    Cars are now 9–19 years old. The 15-year fitness and registration status in the buyer's state is a factual check that asking prices do not include.

06The verdict

India's first home-grown common-rail diesel SUV line — 140 PS and 320 Nm through a manual gearbox, seven seats, and a 4×4 option with low range and a limited-slip differential.
In brief
  • A home-grown common-rail firstThe DICOR line was India's first of its kind
  • Manual only, by design140 PS and 320 Nm through a 5-speed manual
  • Seven seats, spare on the tailgateIn a 4,650 mm ladder-frame body
