03Key benchmarks

  • Performance

    BMW M5 CS

    The fastest M5 built: 635 bhp from a twin-turbo V8, carbon-fibre bucket seats and a chassis tuned beyond the standard M5.

  • Value

    BMW 320d (F30)

    Rear-wheel drive, diesel torque and balanced handling at used-sedan prices — among the most accessible driver's cars in India.

  • Longevity

    Toyota Land Cruiser

    A diesel V8 in a full-size 4×4 renowned for durability, service life and off-road range.

04What this guide covers

  • Dynamics

    Handling and balance

    Priority goes to rear-wheel drive, steering feel and balanced handling. Front-wheel-drive cars are covered when they are known for engaging dynamics — the Octavia vRS and the Abarth Punto are examples.

  • Character

    Distinctive engines

    Coverage favours distinctive engines and engineering — diesel torque, rotary, VTEC and V8s — and cars with physical, analogue controls over screen-heavy designs.

  • Relevance

    Real cars, India context

    Every car covered was sold in India, is available as an import, or is a significant model in Indian motoring. No concept cars.
