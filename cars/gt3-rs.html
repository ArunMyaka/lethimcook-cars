  • Option

    Manthey Performance Kit

    Announced November 2024

    A factory kit adding aero, suspension and brake changes, with no change to the official power output or top speed.

04Watch-outs

  • No front boot

    The central radiator sits where other 911s carry luggage. There is no front luggage compartment — a design decision stated by Porsche itself.

  • Top speed is the trade-off

    The aero package caps top speed at 296 km/h — lower than a standard GT3 — in exchange for 860 kg of downforce at 285 km/h.

  • Carbon and magnesium parts cost

    Weissach-package magnesium wheels and CFRP panels are expensive to replace after kerb or track damage — a documented ownership consideration in buying guides.

  • Power tuning is limited by design

    At 131 PS per litre with no turbocharger, the documented aftermarket focuses on aero, suspension and exhaust rather than engine power. The factory Manthey kit follows the same logic.

06The verdict

860 kg of downforce at 285 km/h — more than half the car's own DIN weight — from a naturally aspirated engine that revs to 9,000 rpm.
