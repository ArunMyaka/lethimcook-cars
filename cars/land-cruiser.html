04Watch-outs

  • CBU pricing throughout

    The LC200 was sold only as a fully imported CBU, which is why it ended its run as India's most expensive Toyota at ₹1.47 crore. Import-route parts pricing follows from that.

  • A BS4 diesel, permanently

    It was discontinued specifically because of the BS6 deadline. Registration and fitness rules for BS4 diesels in applicable cities are a factual check for any buyer.

  • Fuel use

    Published economy figures are poor — 8.1 km/l in aggregator listings, and 5.5 km/l as the manufacturer figure listed for the early car. The 93-litre tank exists because the car needs it.

  • No verified used range

    No reliable published LC200-specific used price range was found in research. Aggregator 'Land Cruiser from ₹13 lakh' lines mix all generations and should not be read as an LC200 figure.

06The verdict

A twin-turbo V8 diesel making 650 Nm from 1,600 rpm, in a full-time-4WD frame SUV with 10 airbags and a 93-litre tank — India's most expensive Toyota.
