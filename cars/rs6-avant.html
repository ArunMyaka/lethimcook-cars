03Variants & the India connection

  • Base

    RS 6 Avant (600 PS)

    2019 – 2025

    The standard C8 car: 600 PS, 800 Nm, 0–100 km/h in 3.6 s, 250 km/h limited top speed.

  • Faster

    RS 6 Avant performance

    630 PS · from 2022

    Output rises to 630 PS and 850 Nm; 0–100 km/h in 3.4 s and a 280 km/h top speed.

  • Limited

    RS 6 Avant GT

    630 PS · 2024

    A limited special edition with the performance engine tune, 0–100 km/h in 3.3 s and a 305 km/h top speed, with weight-saving and aero changes.

  • India

    RS 6 Avant (C7) — sold in India

    ₹1.35 crore — ex-showroom, June 2015

    The previous-generation car was India's first performance estate, sold as a CBU with 560 PS. The later C7 performance version was listed at ₹1.59 crore. The C8 was not officially launched in India.

05Watch-outs

  • Hot-vee coolant leaks

    Specialist guides document coolant pipe and thermostat-housing leaks in the hot-vee engine bay, where heat ages plastic and rubber parts.

  • Turbo wastegate rattle

    A documented EA825 item in specialist problem guides — listen for rattle on cold start and overrun.

  • Carbon build-up

    Direct injection only, with no port injectors to wash the intake valves — carbon build-up over mileage is a known service item on this engine family.

  • 48V system and air suspension

    The 48V battery sits in the boot; air-suspension compressors and struts can leak with age, and a car that sits low after parking is the tell-tale.

  • Consumables cost

    420 mm front discs, 10-piston calipers and wide performance tyres make brakes and tyres a major running cost — check remaining life before buying.

06The verdict

A 600 PS estate that carries 565 litres of luggage with the seats up — the RS 6 Avant pairs supercar pace with a family car's body.
In brief
  • 600 PS and 565 litres, in one carFive seats up, the boot still swallows family luggage
  • 305 km/h is availableThe Dynamic package plus lifts the limiter from 250 to 305 km/h
  • A mild-hybrid twin-turbo V848V assistance and cylinder deactivation on a performance V8
  • 440 mm optional front discsAmong the largest brakes fitted to any production car
  • 630 PS versions followedPerformance (3.4 s) and GT (3.3 s) cut the 0–100 km/h time further
  • India knows the C7Launched at ₹1.35 crore in 2015 as India's first performance estate
