04Watch-outs

  • Real economy trails the claim

    Autocar India's road test measured 10.86 km/l city and 14.74 km/l highway for the manual, against the 16.94 km/l ARAI claim.

  • Vocal at highway speed

    ≈3,200 rpm at 100 km/h in the manual makes it a loud cruiser, and Autocar found the manual's shifts firm and effortful — it preferred the automatic despite slower acceleration.

  • Four seats, small boot

    It is a strict four-seater with 208 litres of boot space seats-up. Five doors do not make it a five-seat family car.

  • A dated automatic

    The 4-speed torque converter is an older design than the 6-speed units in other Maruti models, and it is the slower car (17.47 s tested).

A ladder-frame 4×4 with rigid axles, coil springs and low range as standard on every variant — 3,985 mm long, with official angles of 36°, 24° and 47°.
