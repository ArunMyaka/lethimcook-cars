03Watch-outs

  • Snap-ring failure (1991–92 manuals)

    On early 5-speed manuals, a too-deep groove in the upper transmission case lets the countershaft snap ring break. Affected transmission numbers run from J4A4-1003542 to J4A4-1005978. The correct fix is upper-case replacement, not just a new ring — check the transmission number before buying.

  • Timing belt is critical

    The C30A is an interference engine. Published service intervals vary between sources (60,000–90,000 miles / 7–8 years) — treat belt replacement as time-and-mileage critical and demand proof.

  • Aluminium body repairs

    The all-aluminium structure needs specialists for panel and structural repair. Check for poor accident repairs, which are harder to hide and costlier to correct than on a steel car.

  • Import-only in India

    The NSX was never sold by Honda India. Parts, and technicians who know the car, follow the import route.

04The verdict

The first production car with an all-aluminium body, a VTEC V6 with titanium connecting rods, and chassis input from Ayrton Senna — at 1,170 mm tall and about 1,370 kg.
