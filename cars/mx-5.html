03Watch-outs

  • Early manual gearbox (2015–2018)

    The generation's one well-documented weak point: early cars, especially 2016–2017, suffered 2nd and 3rd gear damage linked to insufficient gear rigidity and heat treatment. Mazda revised the gears, issued technical service bulletins and replaced many gearboxes under warranty. On an early car, check 2nd and 3rd engagement cold and warm, and look for a replacement-gearbox record.

  • Automatic downshift recall

    Automatic cars built from October 2016 to November 2018 could downshift unexpectedly; a software reflash is the documented fix.

  • Import-only in India

    No Mazda sales or service network exists in India. Every ND in the country is a private import, and parts follow that route.

04The verdict

About 100 kg lighter and 105 mm shorter than the car it replaced, the ND won both World Car of the Year and World Car Design of the Year in 2016.
