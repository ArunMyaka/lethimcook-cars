03Watch-outs

  • One model year only

    CS production ran for a single model year. CS-specific trim — Gold Bronze parts, carbon seats, badges — is not shared with the standard M5 and is priced accordingly.

  • Carbon-ceramic replacement cost

    The standard-fit carbon-ceramic brakes are a major replacement cost when worn or damaged. Check disc condition and pad life on any used car.

  • S63 family oil use

    Specialist buying guides for the S63 engine family document valve-stem-seal wear and oil consumption, plus turbo oil and coolant line leaks on earlier versions. The F90's T4 version is the most developed, but oil analysis and full service history are the standard checks.

  • No official India support route

    The CS was never sold by BMW India. Cars in India are private imports, so parts and warranty support follow the import route, not the Indian dealer network.

04The verdict

635 PS, four bucket seats and a single year of production — the M5 CS is the most powerful production BMW M car of its era, in a four-door saloon body.
