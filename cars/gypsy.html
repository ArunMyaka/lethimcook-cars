04Watch-outs

  • Leaf-spring ride

    All-round leaf springs give a harsh, bumpy ride, especially in the rear seats — documented in period reviews and unchanged for the car's whole life.

  • No power steering, no safety kit

    Heavy steering at parking speeds, no airbags and no ABS are inherent to the design, not faults. Buyers should price the car as what it is.

  • Fuel consumption

    Poor economy was a major documented owner complaint; the listed ARAI figure is 11.96 km/l and real use is thirstier.

  • Ex-service auction cars are ELV stock

    Ex-Army and ex-service Gypsys reach the public through government/MSTC disposal auctions categorised as End of Life Vehicles — sold as condemned stock, not as ordinary road-ready used cars. The paperwork route is entirely different from a civilian used car.

06The verdict

An 80 bhp 1.3-litre petrol engine in under 1,050 kg, with a 2.268:1 low range and leaf springs at both ends — a working 4×4 that outlived almost everything else on Indian roads.

  • Leaf springs at both endsDocumented as harsh on the road — and very hard to break
  • No airbags, no ABS, everWhich, with crash norms, is what ended it in 2019
  • One of India's longest production runsThe Gypsy line ran from 1985 to 2019 for the public
  • The Army and police mainstayThe MG413W became their standard light vehicle
