04Watch-outs

  • Gearbox and torque-converter failures

    A dedicated Team-BHP thread documents 3.2 automatic gearbox and torque-converter failures in India, with owners noting recalls in other markets but not India. Any transmission jerk or hesitation on a test drive is a serious flag.

  • Documented owner-reported faults

    Team-BHP's official-review coverage collates owner reports for this generation including EGR-valve check-engine lights, rear main oil-seal leaks and crank-position-sensor faults.

  • Ford's India exit

    Ford ended India manufacturing in 2021. Support continues through Ford's service network for existing owners, but parts and service continuity is a documented consideration for any buyer.

06The verdict

A five-cylinder diesel making 200 PS and 470 Nm, with low range, a rear diff lock and 800 mm of wading depth — tested at 11.2 seconds to 100 km/h despite weighing about 2.4 tonnes.
