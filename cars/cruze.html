04Watch-outs

  • Chevrolet left India in 2017

    Sales ended on 31 December 2017 — the single biggest ownership factor. Chevrolet publicly committed to continued parts and aftersales support through 170+ authorised touchpoints, with genuine parts sold over the counter; in practice, check parts availability for the specific car locally before buying.

  • Takata airbag recall

    The Cruze is covered by the Takata airbag recall. Check any car's VIN against the recall before purchase.

  • The two engines share almost nothing

    SOHC belt-drive (150 PS) against DOHC chain-drive (166 PS), with different displacements. Engine parts are not interchangeable between versions — confirm which engine a car actually has.

  • Weight

    At ≈1,520 kg the Cruze is heavy for its segment. It feels strong in a straight line; it is not a light, agile car, and fuel use follows the weight.

06The verdict

The most powerful diesel sedan in its Indian segment at launch — 166 PS and 380 Nm — in a car whose ownership story is now defined by its maker's 2017 exit.
In brief
  • Segment-topping diesel power166 PS / 380 Nm was the class peak in India at launch
  • The 2012 change was mechanicalDOHC, chain drive, 1,800-bar injection, 6-speed manual
  • A heavy car≈1,520 kg blunts the headline power figure
