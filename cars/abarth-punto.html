04Watch-outs

  • Fiat left India in 2019

    Fiat-branded sales and production ended in early 2019, with after-sales support stated to continue for existing customers. In practice the dealer and service network largely closed or converted to Jeep/Mopar outlets — service depends on ex-Fiat, Jeep-network or specialist support.

  • The gearbox limits the engine

    Period tests documented that the standard Punto's gearbox and 195-section tyres limit how much of the 145 bhp is usable. It is a documented characteristic, not a fault to discover after buying.

  • Low volume, scarce listings

    A single-variant car sold for roughly three years by a brand that has since exited. Listings are scarce, and no published used-price range was found in research — price any car on condition and records, not on a chart.

06The verdict

The first sub-₹10 lakh hatchback in India to claim a sub-10-second 0–100 km/h time — with hydraulic steering and four disc brakes, in a class that had neither.
